---
title: Exchange rates refresh themselves
date: 2026-09-02
type: Feature
tags: Invoicing, Currencies, Subscriptions
summary: Daily rates from the ECB, stamped onto each invoice at issue time — and next renewal's tax on the subscription.
---

**Rates refresh daily from the European Central Bank**, so a multi-currency book converts at
something current without anyone maintaining a table.

**Each invoice stamps the rate it was issued at.** A converted amount on an invoice stays the amount
that was invoiced, whatever rates do afterwards — which is what makes the document a record rather
than a live calculation.

**The subscription page shows next renewal's tax**, so the amount the customer will be charged is
visible before it happens rather than after.
