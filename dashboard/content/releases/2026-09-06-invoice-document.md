The invoice PDF and its on-screen version were rebuilt: clearer hierarchy, a readable line-item
table, totals that add up in front of you, and tax presented the way the place of supply requires.

- **Every invoice states the period it covers**, read from the lines it was billed for — so a
  historical invoice reads as a record of its own period.
- **An invoice can be issued by the organization** rather than a single business, for merchants who
  bill under one legal entity.
- **Countries print as names**, and the document offers what a finance team expects to find on it.
- **Logos reach the PDF**, including SVG.
