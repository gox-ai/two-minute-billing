title: India mandate ceilings, and credit that stays visible
date: 2026-09-04
type: Fix
tags: Payments, India, Customers
summary: The mandate ceiling is set where the gateway reads it, and a customer's credit stays visible after cancellation.
---

- **The India card-mandate ceiling is set on the intent**, which is where the gateway reads it — so
  the amount a mandate is authorised for is the amount that gets registered, and the same card gets
  the same answer every time.
- **Credit stays visible after a cancellation.** Money you owe a customer remains on their page
  whatever happened to the subscription that produced it.
- **PayPal refunds are tagged**, so a refund can be traced back to the payment it reverses.
- **Holds that never captured no longer book a reversal**, keeping the ledger a record of money that
  actually moved.
