---
title: Upload your logo instead of hosting it
date: 2026-09-09
type: Feature
tags: Dashboard, Invoicing, Accounts
summary: Pick a file on Brand styles or Documents and we store it — no public URL to arrange first.
---

Your logo, icon, favicon and invoice signature can now be uploaded straight from settings. Choose
a file and it is stored and served for you, so branding your invoices, checkout, portal and email
no longer waits on somewhere to host an image.

- **Upload or paste — both still work.** The URL field stays exactly where it was, so an image you
  already host anywhere public needs no change.
- **PNG or JPEG for anything that prints, up to 5 MB.** Your logo and signature are embedded in
  invoice PDFs, so those two take PNG or JPEG; icons and favicons also take GIF and WebP. We read
  the file itself to decide what it is rather than trusting its name, so a mislabelled file is
  caught before it reaches your customers.
- **Two places to do it.** Your logo, icon and favicon live on Settings → Brand styles; the
  signature that prints on invoices and credit notes lives on Settings → Documents.
- **Using an SVG today? Upload a PNG or JPEG to see it on your invoices.** Invoice PDFs carry
  PNG and JPEG; an SVG still shows on your portal, checkout and email, but the PDF prints your
  business name in place of the mark. Uploading takes a few seconds and fixes it everywhere.

Every surface that shows your brand — the self-serve portal, hosted checkout, invoice PDFs and
billing email — picks up the new image as soon as you save.
