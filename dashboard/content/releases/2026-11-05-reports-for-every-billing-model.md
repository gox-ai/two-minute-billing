Business Home and Reports now show how your business earns money: subscriptions, usage, prepaid
or AI credits, and one-time sales.

- **Revenue mix.** The Overview shows what you invoiced in the period, by type: subscriptions,
  usage, one-time sales and credit packs. Each type also shows the money that you collected.
- **Credits tab.** See the credit that customers hold and when it expires. See the value of paid
  credit that customers did not use yet. See credit granted, used and expired in the period, and
  the revenue that it becomes.
- **Usage tab.** See usage revenue, events and customers by meter, the cost of usage, and usage
  revenue retention. A count shows the customers who stopped sending usage.
- **One-time sales tab.** See orders, order revenue by product, average order value, returning
  buyers and refunds on orders.
- **New dashboards.** Reports has a Credits, a Usage and a One-time sales dashboard.
- **New customer segments.** Low credit balance, Credits expiring soon, Usage stopped, Repeat
  buyers, One-time buyers and No order in 90 days.

## Only what applies to your business

A tab or a dashboard shows only when your business has that kind of data in the last 90 days.
If your business has no subscriptions, the Overview starts with revenue of all types, and Reports
opens on your own dashboard.
