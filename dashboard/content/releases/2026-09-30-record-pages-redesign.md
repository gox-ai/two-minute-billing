Every detail page now follows one layout: what needs your attention first, the key figures next,
then the record itself in clear sections. It applies to customers, subscriptions, invoices, credit
notes, transactions, payment intents, tax regions, products, prices, product groups,
coupons and coupon sets.

## Act on what needs you

- **Needs attention sits at the top.** An unpaid invoice shows how long it has been due, how many
  attempts were made and when the next retry is, with a timeline of every attempt and the retries
  still to come. Hover a step for the card and the decline reason.
- **Collect payment lets you choose.** On a customer, tick the unpaid invoices to collect; on an
  invoice or subscription, pick the card to charge. A payment you collect by hand never uses up
  one of the scheduled retries.
- **Invoices within their terms stay calm.** An invoice that is issued but not yet due is not
  flagged; Collect payment is still in More if a customer wants to pay early.

## Know your catalog

- **See what each plan earns.** Products, prices and product groups show their active
  subscriptions and MRR, per currency.
- **See what each coupon has given.** Coupons show every redemption and the total discount given;
  coupon sets show which subscription redeemed each code, can be renamed, and export their codes
  as CSV.
- **Limit redemptions per customer.** A coupon can now be capped at a number of uses per customer.
- **Activity and comments on catalog records.** Changes to products, prices, families, coupons and
  coupon sets appear in the Activity log, and your team can leave comments on them.

## Forms that preview the result

- **Create a plan with its price in one go.** New plans, add-ons and charges take their first price
  point on the same page, and every form shows a live summary such as "Customers pay $19.80 per seat
  every month, with a 14-day free trial."
- **Every option on one form.** Tiers, trials, billing cycles, tax behaviour and metadata are all
  editable where you create or edit the record. Fields that cannot change after creation say why.

## Easier to read

- **Long lists page instead of scrolling.** History, activity, comments, codes and redemptions show
  10 or 5 at a time, newest first, with every record reachable.
- **Tables keep their shape.** Columns stay fixed as rows appear, and long text wraps instead of
  pushing the table sideways.
