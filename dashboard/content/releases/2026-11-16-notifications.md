You can now get a notification when something in your billing needs a person. Each member of
your team selects their own events, and each event goes to the bell, to your devices, to your
email, or into a daily summary.

- **Select what you want to know.** 26 events: failed payments, disputes and their evidence
  deadlines, retries that ran out, cancellations, spending limits, webhook problems and more.
  Open your account menu and select **Notifications**.
- **Instant, daily summary or off, per event.** An instant event shows in the bell at once and on
  the devices and email you select. A summary event waits for your daily summary.
- **Only the amounts that matter.** Set a limit per currency, for example payments above
  ₹10,000 only.
- **One setup for all your businesses.** Your settings apply to every business, also to new ones.
  Give one business different settings, or limit notifications to the businesses you select.
- **No flood during a renewal run.** Many events of the same type in five minutes become one
  notification: "11 more payments failed".
- **Quiet hours.** Push notifications wait until the morning. Dispute notifications still come,
  because a dispute has a deadline.

## A daily and a weekly summary

The daily summary shows what needs action first, then yesterday's figures compared with the same
day last week, your summary events, and what comes next: renewals, trials that end and cards that
expire. The weekly summary shows your MRR movement, collected, failed and recovered payments, and
cancellations by reason.

## Google Chat and outside email addresses

Owners and admins can send notifications to a Google Chat space or to an email address outside
the team, such as your accountant. Open **Settings**, then **Team notifications**. Each space or
address gets its own businesses, events and limits. Team alerts are now part of notifications:
set up your notifications to continue to get them.

## Install Two Minute Billing as an app

Open your account menu and select **Install the app**. The app opens in its own window and gets
push notifications on your desktop and on your phone. On an iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen
from Safari first. The app does not keep billing data on your device.
