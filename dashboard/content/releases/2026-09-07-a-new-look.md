The dashboard has a visual direction of its own.

- **Cards sit on a tinted ground.** Every panel, table and form reads as a distinct surface, which
  makes a long page much easier to scan.
- **One accent colour** carries both links and the primary action on a screen, so what to do next is
  always obvious.
- **Colour means something.** A state that needs a human — payment due, failed — keeps a filled
  badge. Healthy and finished states render quiet, so a list of mostly-cancelled subscriptions reads
  calm rather than alarming.
- **Softer corners, roomier rows, and a 12px floor on type**, so nothing on a screen you read all day
  is smaller than that.
- **Charts follow the theme**, in light and dark.

## The sidebar collapses

The navigation rail shrinks to an icon strip and remembers your choice, giving wide tables back
around 240px. Press the handle on its edge to switch.
