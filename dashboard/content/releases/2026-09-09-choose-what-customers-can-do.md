---
title: Choose what customers can do in the portal
date: 2026-09-09
type: Feature
tags: Settings, Portal, Accounts
summary: Three new switches on Hosted pages decide whether customers can download invoices, pay them, and remove a saved payment method.
---

Settings → Hosted pages has a new section: **What customers can do in the portal**. Three
switches, all on unless you turn them off, so nothing changes until you decide it should.

- **Let customers download their invoices.** Turning it off keeps the invoice visible — a
  customer still sees what they were billed — but removes the PDF download.
- **Let customers pay an unpaid invoice.** This removes the customer's own Pay now only. We
  still collect on your schedule and dunning still runs, so turning it off does not stop the
  money arriving. Useful if you collect strictly by mandate or bank transfer and a second,
  manual path would muddle your reconciliation.
- **Let customers remove a payment method.** Adding one is always allowed; this governs
  removal. Worth turning off where a live subscription depends on the card or mandate on
  file — nothing else prevents a customer removing it.
- **Let customers pause or resume their own subscription.** Turning it off keeps the subscription active
  even if the customer would like to cancel. Worth turning off where a live subscription depends on
  the customer's subscription — nothing else prevents a customer canceling it.
- **Let customers cancel their own subscription.** Turning it off keeps the subscription active
  even if the customer would like to cancel. Worth turning off where a live subscription depends on
  the customer's subscription — nothing else prevents a customer canceling it.

Each switch applies to the self-serve portal only, and takes effect immediately for every
customer of that business.
