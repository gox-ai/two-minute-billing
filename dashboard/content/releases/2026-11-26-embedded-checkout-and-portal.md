Customers can now subscribe and manage their billing without leaving your app.

- **Checkout over your page.** The checkout opens in a panel over your app. On a phone it fills the
  screen. The customer closes it with the close button or the Escape key.
- **Checkout in your layout.** Put the checkout in your own pricing page. It takes the height of
  its content.
- **Customer portal over your page.** Customers change their plan, add a payment method and pay
  invoices in a panel over your app.
- **Events for your app.** Your page gets an event when the customer pays, changes a plan or
  adds a payment method. Your app can then show the new plan at once.
- **Only your websites.** Add your websites under **Settings → Checkout → Embed on your
  website**. Other websites cannot show your checkout or portal. The usage widget uses the same
  list.
- **Payments that need another page.** PayPal and bank pages open in a new tab. The customer
  approves there and continues in your app.

Read [Embed checkout and the portal](/help/developers/recipes/embed-checkout-and-portal) for the
steps and the code.
