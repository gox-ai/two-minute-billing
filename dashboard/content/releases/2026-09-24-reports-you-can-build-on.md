**Reports** is now its own section: close to a hundred reports across recurring revenue, customers,
billing, cash, receivables, tax and collections, with charts, breakdowns and CSV export.

## Boards you arrange

- **Start from a built-in board** — five of them, so the section is useful on day one.
- **Build your own** by adding widgets: pick a type (a single figure, a ranked breakdown or a
  table), the measure, and how to split it, over a preset or custom period.
- **Compare with the previous period** on any figure, in every currency the business bills in.

## Segments

- **Save a group of customers as a segment** and ask MRR, its movements or customer counts
  for just that group.
- **Split a figure by country or plan** to see which part of the book moved it.

## A history you can trust

- **Past MRR is what each subscription actually earned that day.** An upgrade, a discount or a
  reactivation lands on the day it happened, and every earlier day keeps the figure it had.
- **A trial counts once it converts**, and a cancelled account keeps the rate it was paying
  until the day it left.
- **Movements add up.** New, expansion, reactivation, contraction and churn sum exactly to the
  change in MRR, month after month.

## Every currency, at the rate of the day

- **Converted figures use the exchange rate that applied on the day**, from the European Central
  Bank's daily reference rates, so a figure from two years ago is stated at that year's rate.
- **Currency moves have their own line** in the MRR movement report, so a weaker or stronger
  currency never reads as growth or churn.

## Faster, and easier to find

- **The business home answers one period on every tab**, with eight presets and a custom range.
- **Subscriptions read as Active, Paying and Renewing** — everything live, what carries revenue,
  and what is set to renew — side by side on both homes, with a Renewing report of its own.
- **See collections at a glance**: the business home shows how many invoices are in dunning, and
  the businesses list has a Dunning column.
- **Reports and the business home load faster** on large books.
- **The organization sidebar lists your businesses**, so you can jump straight to one.
