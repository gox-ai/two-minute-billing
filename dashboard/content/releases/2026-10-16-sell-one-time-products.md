The hosted checkout now sells one-time products, not only subscriptions. When all the prices in a
checkout are one-time prices, the buyer pays once and gets a paid invoice. No subscription is
created. When a checkout has a recurring price, it continues to create a subscription.

- **One-time products.** The catalog shows them under **One-time products**. On a product page,
  the **Hosted checkout** card makes a checkout link for a one-time price.
- **Payment links.** Under **Payment links**, make one link that many buyers can use. Put it on
  your website or in an email. Each buyer gets their own checkout, customer record and invoice.
- **A public pricing page.** Show your products and prices on one page that buyers can open
  without a login.
- **Quantities.** On a payment link, you can let the buyer change the quantity, between the
  limits that you set.
- **Save the payment method.** In **Hosted pages → One-time purchases**, select if the checkout
  saves the buyer's payment method: never, always, or the buyer decides.

## A faster checkout

- **One page.** The buyer's details and the payment step show on one page. On a phone, the order
  lines fold to make the page shorter.
- **The total first.** The checkout shows the amount due today, with tax, before the buyer pays.
- **Faster to open.** The checkout loads the card form only when the buyer needs it.

## After the purchase

- **Purchases in the customer portal.** Buyers see their one-time purchases, each with a link to
  its receipt.
- **Payments that the bank must still confirm.** Sometimes a payment provider holds a payment to
  check it. We keep the payment and record it when the provider confirms it. In the customer
  portal, the buyer sees **Payment in progress**, and **Pay now** is not available for that
  invoice until the payment completes.

## Bring buyers back

- **Checkout reminders.** In **Emails**, switch on **Checkout reminder**. A buyer who gives an
  email address and does not complete the checkout gets a reminder after 1 hour, and a second
  reminder after 24 hours. The link in the email opens the same checkout. This email is off until
  you switch it on.
- **Two new reports.** In **Reports → Acquisitions**, **Checkout Reminders** shows how many buyers
  got a reminder and how many completed their checkout after it. **Recovered Checkout Revenue**
  shows the money from those purchases.
