---
title: The billing clock has a diary
date: 2026-09-02
type: Feature
tags: Ops
summary: A due-date index you can read, maintained as work is scheduled — plus fair scheduling across tenants.
---

**`/ops/due-work` is the billing clock's diary.** Everything the clock is going to do, when it is
going to do it, and what is overdue — on one screen, readable at a glance.

- The diary is **maintained as work is written**, so it is accurate the moment you open it rather
  than as of the last sweep.
- **The tick's pass list is declared and enforced**, so what the clock does each cycle is a stated
  contract rather than an emergent one.
- **No tenant can starve another.** Work is scheduled fairly across businesses, so one large book
  does not delay everyone else's renewals.
- A healthy tick is reported as healthy, so the alerts that fire are the ones worth reading.
