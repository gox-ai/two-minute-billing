---
title: The billing clock got faster
date: 2026-09-02
type: Performance
tags: Ops, Payments
summary: Settlement sweeps were the bulk of every cycle; they are now a small part of it.
---

Settlement sweeps accounted for the overwhelming majority of each billing tick. They have been
scoped to the work that actually needs doing, which gives every cycle back to the work you care
about — renewals, retries and invoicing.

The practical effect: the clock keeps up comfortably at your current book size, with room as it
grows.
