Billy is the assistant in your dashboard. Ask a question in plain words. Billy reads your data
with your own access and gives you the answer, with links to the screens where you can act.

- **Ask from anywhere.** Click **Ask Billy** at the top of the left menu, or press ⌘J. On an
  invoice, customer, subscription or payment, click **Ask about this** to start with that record.
  In search (⌘K), type a question and select the first row.
- **Every figure is checked.** Billy compares each amount and date in the answer with your data.
  A green line tells you that Billy found every figure. A figure that Billy could not find has a
  dotted underline.
- **Records, not raw data.** Invoices, customers, subscriptions and payments show as cards and
  small tables with links. Metric answers show a small chart.
- **Questions with options.** When an answer depends on a choice, Billy asks one question at a
  time. Select an option or press 1–5. Your business's default is selected.
- **Tools in the input.** Use ＋ to attach a screenshot, mention a record, or start a task such
  as Explain an invoice. Type @ to find a customer, invoice or subscription.
- **After each answer.** Copy the answer, tell us if it helped, ask again, or select a follow-up
  question.

## Changes that you confirm

An owner or admin can let Billy take actions in **Settings → Billy**. Billy then prepares a
change, shows you what will change, and does nothing until you click **Confirm**. Billy uses your
own permissions, and the record's history shows that you made the change through Billy.

Money actions, such as refunds and voiding an invoice, have a separate switch. Each one asks you
to confirm a second time.
