Each product can now have an image. Buyers see it next to the product on the hosted checkout, on
payment links, on your pricing page and in their purchases in the customer portal.

- **Add an image.** On the new or edit page of a product, select an image: PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP,
  up to 5 MB. You see a preview before you save.
- **See what buyers see.** On a one-time product, the summary shows the product as the checkout
  shows it: the image, the name, the description and the price with its tax.
- **Send buyers to your page after payment.** Enter an **After-payment page**, for example a
  thank-you or download page. Checkout sends buyers there after they pay, if the checkout link or
  payment link has no page of its own.

## One-time products are easier to set up

- **How you sell it.** One card shows the two ways to sell a one-time product: on its own with a
  checkout link or payment link, or with a subscription, for example as a setup fee.
- **SKU and accounting codes.** Enter them with the first price.
- **Taxable.** Disable it for an item that has no tax, for example a gift card.
- **Next step.** After you create a one-time product, its page opens at its payment links.
- **Product ID.** If you leave the ID empty, we make one from the name.
