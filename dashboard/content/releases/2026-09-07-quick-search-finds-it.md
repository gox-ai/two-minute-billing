---
title: ⌘K finds a customer by phone or company
date: 2026-09-07
type: Fix
tags: Dashboard, Accounts, Invoicing
summary: Quick search matches every field it says it does, across every row — not just the page in front of it.
---

Press ⌘K inside a business, type whatever you remember about a record, and it comes back.

- **Phone and company find a customer.** Quick search matches the same fields the customer
  list does — id, email, first and last name, company and phone — so the thing you happen to
  have in front of you is enough to find the account.
- **Subscriptions and invoices search the whole list**, however long it has grown, and return
  the closest five of each alongside the customers.
- **A search in progress says so.** The palette reads "Searching…" while it is looking, and
  only reports nothing found once it knows.
