Reports and Home now fit the way each business earns money. In the portal, customers pay an
invoice and keep the payment method in the same step.

## Reports for the way you sell

- **Sales report.** **Reports → Sales** shows orders, order revenue, average order value, buyers,
  returning buyers, refunds and the refund rate. See units and revenue by product, first-time
  and returning buyers, and discounts. See order revenue by country, payment gateway and coupon.
- **Orders list.** **Orders** in the sidebar shows every order for your one-time products. See
  the customer, the products, the amount, the status and the payment method. Filter by status,
  product, coupon, gateway or country, and add more columns. Each order has its own page with the
  items, the payment and the refunds. Refund an order from there.
- **Do buyers come back.** Two cohort tables show repeat purchases and revenue per buyer, grouped
  by the month of the first order.
- **Usage report.** The Usage margin report is now **Usage**. See units by meter, the top
  consumers and the customers who stopped. See how each customer is doing against a minimum
  commitment, and the shortfall. The margin is still there.
- **Credits report.** See the burn rate and how long customers' credit lasts, in bands from 7
  days or less to more than 90 days. See packs sold, pack sales and the revenue recognized.
- **Home tabs for each model.** Business Home has a Credits, a Usage and a One-time sales tab.
  See the credit that customers hold, the usage revenue by meter, and the orders and refunds.
- **Revenue mix.** The Overview shows what you invoiced in the period, by type: subscriptions,
  usage, one-time sales and credit packs. Each type also shows the money that you collected.
- **Dashboards.** Reports has a Revenue dashboard for all types, and a Credits, a Usage and a
  One-time sales dashboard.
- **Only what applies.** A tab or a dashboard shows only when the business has that kind of data
  in the last 90 days. The Reports menu, Stats and Explore follow the same rule, and Stats and
  Explore are grouped by model. If a business has no subscriptions, the Overview starts with
  revenue of all types.
- **Checkout report by product.** Filter the report by any product. A checkout counts when any
  item in it is that product, for example an add-on, a one-time charge or a credit pack.
- **New customer segments.** Low credit balance, Credits expiring soon, Usage stopped, Repeat
  buyers, One-time buyers and No order in 90 days. Segments also show revenue billed.
- **Two lifetime figures.** The old figure is now **Subscription lifetime value**. The new
  **Lifetime revenue per customer** counts revenue of all types.

## A home that fits every business

- **Organization home.** Each business card shows the figure that fits it: MRR, or revenue in
  the last 30 days for a business with orders, usage or credits. The totals show the same MRR,
  paying customers, churn and trial conversion cards as a business. Revenue of every type shows
  below them. A business shows "No price" only when it has no price.
- **Business Home.** If a business has subscriptions and other models, the cards for orders,
  usage and credits show under MRR.
- **Setup steps.** The steps end when the business has any price and any first bill. You can
  close them.

## Checkout and customer portal

- **Pay and save in one step.** In the portal, customers select **Pay now**. They pay with a
  saved method or a new one, and can keep the new method for later invoices. We record their
  consent. In **Settings → Hosted pages**, select **Ask the customer**, **Always** or **Never**.
- **A failed payment is explained.** The portal shows the reason for the last failed payment. If
  the same method cannot succeed, it asks for a new one first.
- **See what the new method paid.** After a customer adds a method, a dialog shows each open
  invoice: paid, in progress, not collected with the reason, or still due.
- **Checkout, Portal and Emails reports.** See where buyers come from and where they leave
  checkout. See the payment methods and the failures. See what customers do in the portal. See
  email delivery, and opens and clicks when **Track opens and clicks** is on in **Settings → Emails**. Each report
  compares with the previous period and exports to CSV.
