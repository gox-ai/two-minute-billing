title: Resuming a subscription bills exactly what it should
date: 2026-09-27
type: Fix
tags: Subscriptions, Invoicing
summary: A resume inside a paid term carries on at no charge; a resume after it starts and bills a new term.
---

When you resume a paused subscription, what it bills now depends on one question: has the term
your customer already paid for ended?

- **Still inside the paid term:** the subscription carries on to the same renewal date and nothing
  new is charged. Your customer keeps the time they paid for.
- **After the paid term ended:** a new term starts on the day of the resume and is billed for in
  full, just like a renewal.

## Choose how the new term is billed

- **Invoice immediately** raises and collects the new term's invoice at the moment of resume.
  It is the default unless you have chosen otherwise.
- **Add to unbilled charges** holds the charge and bills it with the next invoice instead.
- **Set your default** in Settings → Billing rules → Pause and resume. The Resume dialog preselects
  it, and you can still pick the other option for a single resume.
- **The same rule applies everywhere:** resumes from the dashboard, the API, your customers'
  self-serve portal, and pauses that resume automatically after a set number of cycles.
