Two Minute Billing is subscription billing software that charges through your own payment gateway.
These docs cover both ways to use it, on Two Minute Billing Cloud or on your own servers:
[getting started](/help/getting-started) and the [operator guides](/help/guides) for running billing
in the dashboard, the [developer docs](/help/developers) for the REST API, webhooks and recipes, and
the [API reference](/help/developers/api-reference) for every endpoint.
