Notifications tell you and your team when something in your billing needs a person: a payment
failed, a dispute opened, a customer's retries ran out. Each member chooses what reaches them and
how. Owners and admins can also send notifications to a shared Google Chat space or to an email
address outside your team, such as your accountant.

## Set up your notifications

Open your account menu and select **Notifications** (or select **Notification settings** at the
foot of the bell panel). Nothing reaches you until you save this page once; the first time, it
starts from sensible defaults for your role, so you can review and save.

For each event, set **When**:

- **Instant** — it shows in the bell at once, and on the devices and email you tick.
- **Daily summary** — it waits for your daily summary instead of interrupting you.
- **Off** — you do not get it.

Events with an amount have **Only above**: set an amount per currency, and smaller payments,
refunds or invoices do not notify you. A currency with no amount set notifies for every amount.

Your settings apply to **every business** in the organization, including businesses you create
later. To hear about some businesses only, change **Notify me about** to **Selected businesses**.
To treat one business differently — for example, every failed payment for your largest business
but only a summary for the others — select **Set different settings for a business**. Only the
rows you change apply to that business; the rest follow **Default**.

You only get events your role can see. If your role cannot view disputes, dispute events are not
offered to you and are not sent to you.

## Events you can get

| Group | Events |
|---|---|
| Payments | Payment failed, payment received, refund failed, payment needs attention, late payment failure, possible fraud |
| Disputes | Dispute opened, evidence due soon (3 days and 1 day before the deadline), dispute won or lost |
| Collections | All payment retries failed, invoice overdue, payment method no longer works |
| Subscriptions | New revenue, new subscription, subscription cancelled, cancellation scheduled, subscription reactivated, trial ends soon, changed by the customer in the portal, large renewal soon |
| Usage and credits | Spending limit reached, usage alert, automatic top-up failed, large free credit grant |
| Operations | Webhook endpoint failing, payment gateway needs attention |

## Push notifications on your phone and desktop

On **Notifications**, select **Enable on this device** and allow notifications when your browser
asks. Do this on each device you want notifications on — your laptop and your phone are separate.
**Send a test notification** checks that it works.

A burst does not flood your lock screen. When several events of the same kind happen for one
business within five minutes — a renewal run with many failed payments, for example — you get the
first one at once and then one notification for the rest ("11 more payments failed").

**Quiet hours** hold push notifications at night, in your own timezone, and send one summary when
they end. Email, the bell and Google Chat are not held. Dispute notifications come through during
quiet hours unless you turn that off, because a dispute has a deadline.

## Install the app

Two Minute Billing installs as an app on your desktop and phone, with its own window, icon and
shortcuts.

- **Chrome, Edge and Android:** open your account menu and select **Install the app**, or use the
  install button in the browser's address bar.
- **iPhone and iPad:** in Safari, select the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open the
  app from your Home Screen and enable notifications there — on iPhone and iPad, push works only in
  the installed app (iOS 16.4 or later).

The app does not keep your billing data on the device. Without a connection it shows an offline
page until you are back online.

## The bell

The bell in the navigation shows the last 90 days of your instant notifications for the
organization you are signed in to, with a count of unread ones. Select a notification to open the
invoice, dispute or customer it is about. Test businesses' events show here with a **Test** label
and are never sent by push, email or Google Chat.

## Daily and weekly summary

The daily summary arrives at the time you choose (08:00 by default, in your timezone) and covers
the day before for each business:

1. **Needs action** — payments with no result from the gateway, disputes with evidence due within
   seven days, invoices whose retries all failed, gateway and webhook problems.
2. **Yesterday in figures** — collected, failed, refunded, new and cancelled subscriptions, MRR
   change, each compared with the same day last week.
3. **Your summary events** — the events you set to **Daily summary**.
4. **Coming up** — renewals in the next seven days, trials that end, cards that expire soon.

The weekly summary compares last week with the week before: payments collected, failed and
recovered by retries, MRR movement by type, and cancellations by reason. A day with no activity
sends nothing unless you turn on **Send on a day with no activity**.

## Google Chat and outside email addresses

Owners and admins open **Settings → Team notifications** to send notifications to places the whole
team reads.

**Add a Google Chat space:** in Google Chat, open the space, select **Apps & integrations**, then
**Webhooks**. Name the webhook "Two Minute Billing" — Google Chat shows that name on every message
— and copy its URL. In Team notifications select **Add space**, paste the URL and select **Send
test and add**. A test message goes to the space first; if Google Chat refuses it, the space is not
added. Each space has its own businesses, events and amount limits, a switch for the daily and
weekly summary, and **Hide customer names and email addresses** for spaces that people outside your
billing team can read.

If a space stops accepting messages — someone deleted the webhook, for example — it shows **Not
working** after five failed sends, and owners and admins get one email. Select **Change webhook
URL** to fix it.

**Add an outside email address:** select **Add address**. The address gets a confirmation email
and receives nothing until someone selects the link in it.

## Team alerts are now notifications

Notifications replace team alerts. Team alert settings were not carried over: set up your
notifications, and add any outside addresses you used in Team notifications. The team alerts API
field still works for now and is deprecated; see the
[businesses API reference](/help/developers/api-reference/businesses/update-a-business).
