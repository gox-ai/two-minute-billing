Two Minute Billing hosts two pages for your customers, both carrying your branding.

| Page | Your customer can |
|---|---|
| **Checkout** | Select a plan, enter their details and pay |
| **Portal** | Update their card, see invoices, and whatever else you allow |

Card details go straight to your payment provider. They never reach your servers, which keeps you
out of the compliance scope that storing card numbers would put you in.

## Set what customers can do

**Settings → Hosted pages** controls the portal. Every switch you turn on is a support ticket your
team stops receiving. Every switch you leave off is a job they keep doing.

Worth enabling for most businesses: updating a payment method, downloading invoices, and seeing
what is owed. Whether customers can change plan or cancel on their own is a commercial decision
rather than a technical one.

## Send a customer a link

Generate the link from the customer or subscription, and send it to them.

::warning{title="The link is single use and expires in an hour"}
Opening it spends it. So do not email the same link twice, and do not put it behind a link checker
or a mail scanner that follows links — the scanner opens it and your customer gets an expired
page.

Generate a fresh one whenever a customer asks. It costs nothing.
::

## Brand them

Under **Settings**, set your logo, icon and brand colour. Check the preview before you send anyone
a link — these pages are where a customer hands over a card, and one that does not look like you
is one they abandon.
