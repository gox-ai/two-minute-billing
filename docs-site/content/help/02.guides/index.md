These guides are for the person who runs the billing: a founder, a finance lead or an operations
team. Each guide is one job, done in the dashboard, with no code. To build billing into your
product with the API, see the [developer docs](/help/developers). New to Two Minute Billing? Start
with [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

## Day to day

- [Customers and subscriptions](/help/guides/customers-and-subscriptions): add customers,
  subscribe them, change what they pay, and cancel.
- [Invoices and credit notes](/help/guides/invoices-and-credit-notes): read an invoice, collect or
  record a payment, request payment for several invoices at once, keep numbered receipts, and
  correct an invoice with a credit note.
- [Recover failed payments](/help/guides/recover-failed-payments): set a retry schedule, decide
  what happens when it runs out, and give some customers their own dunning policy.
- [Handle disputes](/help/guides/handle-disputes): see each chargeback, what happens to the invoice,
  and the evidence you need to respond to the bank.

## Setting up

- [Connect a payment gateway](/help/guides/connect-a-payment-gateway): connect your own payment
  gateway account, so payments go to your bank at your rates.
- [Set up tax](/help/guides/set-up-tax): charge tax where you are registered, inclusive or
  exclusive, with exemptions.
- [Hosted checkout and portal](/help/guides/hosted-checkout-and-portal): pages with your brand
  where customers subscribe, pay, update their card and see their invoices.
- [Payment methods your customers can use](/help/guides/payment-methods): cards and 3D Secure,
  Apple Pay and Google Pay, bank debits, and the mandates and notices that go with them.
- [Emails and branding](/help/guides/emails-and-branding): the address your billing email comes
  from, which emails go out, their wording, and your brand on invoices.
- [Connect your other tools](/help/guides/connect-integrations): sync HubSpot both ways, and send
  sales and refunds to FirstPromoter for your affiliate program.

## Usage and AI billing

- [Price usage and commitments](/help/guides/usage-and-ai-billing/usage-pricing): tiered, volume
  and package pricing on usage, monthly usage on annual plans, and minimum commitments.
- [Meters and rate cards](/help/guides/usage-and-ai-billing/meters-and-rate-cards): count usage
  events with meters and bill a rate for each model and token type.
- [Bill from the AI model price list](/help/guides/usage-and-ai-billing/model-price-list): bill AI
  models at list price or cost plus a markup, and see your margin.
- [Prepaid credits](/help/guides/usage-and-ai-billing/prepaid-credits): sell credit packs, include
  credits in plans, and block usage when credit runs out.
- [Spend limits, alerts and automatic top-up](/help/guides/usage-and-ai-billing/spend-limits-and-auto-top-up):
  cap and watch usage spend, and top up credit before it runs out.

## Running it well

- [Test with Time Travel](/help/guides/test-with-time-travel): move the clock of a test business
  forward to watch renewals, trial ends and retries in seconds.
- [Read your numbers](/help/guides/read-your-numbers): how MRR, ARR, churn and subscriber mix are
  calculated, per currency and per business.
- [Your team, roles and logs](/help/guides/your-team): a fixed or custom role for each person,
  API keys with only the access they need, and a record of who changed what and when.
