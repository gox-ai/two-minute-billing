Two Minute Billing will charge subscriptions through your CyberSource account:

- The customer types the card number and security code into CyberSource's own fields on the
  payment page. Two Minute Billing never sees the card number.
- CyberSource keeps the card in its Token Management Service. Two Minute Billing charges each
  renewal against that saved card.
- Refunds go back to the customer through CyberSource, in full or in part.
- Two Minute Billing registers the webhook with CyberSource for you.

## Chargebacks

CyberSource does not send chargebacks as webhook events. They are in its daily Chargeback and
Retrieval Detail report, and Two Minute Billing reads that report once a day.

1. In the Business Center, create a report subscription for the **Chargeback and Retrieval
   Detail** report.
2. Name it `tmbilling_chargebacks`. Select the CSV format, a daily frequency and the UTC time zone.

A chargeback then shows in **Disputes** the day after CyberSource reports it. When CyberSource
reverses one, the dispute changes to won. When you lose one, open it in **Disputes** and click
**Mark as lost**.

You do not have to wait for the report. Open the payment in **Transactions**, click **Record
dispute** and enter the case number. When the report shows the same case, we update that dispute
and do not record it again.
