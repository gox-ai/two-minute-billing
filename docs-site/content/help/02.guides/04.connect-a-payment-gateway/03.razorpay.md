Two Minute Billing collects recurring rupee payments through **your** Razorpay account: cards,
UPI Autopay and netbanking eMandate. The customer approves a mandate once, and Two Minute Billing
charges each renewal against it. You keep your Razorpay rates, and Razorpay settles to your bank
as usual.

## Before you start

- A Razorpay account. Test mode works at once; live mode needs Razorpay to check your website
  details first.
- Razorpay gives you two sets of keys: **test** and **live**. Connect the test keys to a test
  business and the live keys to a live business.
- Only the Owner and Admin roles in Razorpay can see API keys.
- Recurring card payments need Razorpay to enable them on your account. Ask Razorpay support
  before you offer cards. UPI Autopay and netbanking eMandate do not need this.
- A customer needs a phone number to save a payment method on Razorpay. Razorpay refuses a
  mandate without one.
- You need permission to manage payment gateways in Two Minute Billing. The owner and admin
  roles have it.

## Find your API keys

In the Razorpay Dashboard, select **Test** or **Live** mode to match the business you are
connecting. Go to **Account & Settings → Websites & API keys** and click
**Generate Key**.

::warning
**Copy the key secret when you generate it.** Razorpay shows the key secret only once. If you lose it, generate new keys and update the gateway
account. Anyone with the key secret can charge and refund in your Razorpay account: paste it only
into Two Minute Billing, which stores it encrypted, and never into an email or a chat.
::

## Permissions

::warning
**Razorpay keys have no permission settings.** A Razorpay key can do everything in the account, so there is nothing to select on the key. Check
the items below instead: a missing one does not show when you connect, only later when a mandate
or a renewal fails.
::

- **Recurring card payments.** Razorpay must enable them on your account: ask Razorpay support
  before you offer cards. UPI Autopay and netbanking eMandate are on by default.
- **Customer phone numbers.** Razorpay needs the customer's phone number for a recurring payment.
- **Webhook events.** Two Minute Billing registers the webhook and selects the events for you. If
  you add it by hand, select every event in the list under [Webhooks](#webhooks): Razorpay has no
  "all events" option, and a missing event means a missed payment, refund or dispute.

## Connect it in Two Minute Billing

1. Open **Settings → Payment gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Select **Razorpay** and paste the **Key ID** and the **Key Secret**.
3. Select the **currencies** and the **payment methods** this account handles.
4. Click **Create gateway account**.

Then open the account and click **Check credentials**. **Healthy** means Razorpay accepted the
keys.

The payment methods you can turn on for Razorpay:

<!-- generated:methods -->
- **Cards**
- **UPI Autopay** — INR only. The debit settles asynchronously.
- **Netbanking (eMandate)** — INR only.
<!-- /generated:methods -->

Each mandate has a per-charge limit that the customer approves. Two Minute Billing sets it from
the plan price, with room for growth. UPI Autopay allows at most ₹1,00,000 per charge. A card
charge above ₹15,000 needs the customer to approve it again, so it cannot be collected
automatically. Netbanking eMandate does not have the ₹15,000 limit and allows up to
₹10,00,000 per charge.

## Webhooks

Razorpay tells Two Minute Billing about things that happen after a charge: a UPI or netbanking
debit that settles later, a mandate that is confirmed, paused or cancelled, a refund, a dispute.

<!-- generated:webhooks -->
Two Minute Billing registers the webhook with Razorpay for you when you save the account. Nothing to copy.

If the gateway account page shows the webhook as not configured:

1. In Razorpay, go to Account & Settings → Website and app settings → Webhooks. Click Add New Webhook.
2. Paste the notification URL and the secret on the gateway account page into the URL and Secret fields.
3. In Active Events, select these events: payment.captured, payment.authorized, order.paid, payment.failed, refund.processed, refund.failed, refund.created, payment.dispute.created, payment.dispute.won, payment.dispute.lost, payment.dispute.closed, payment.dispute.action_required, payment.dispute.under_review, token.confirmed, token.rejected, token.cancelled, token.paused, settlement.processed. Other events are not necessary.
4. Click Create Webhook.
<!-- /generated:webhooks -->

## Test it

In your test business, create a customer with a phone number and save a payment method for it:

- **UPI Autopay:** enter the UPI ID `success@razorpay`. Use `failure@razorpay` to see a failure.
- **Card:** use the Razorpay subscription test card `4718 6091 0820 4366` with any future expiry
  date and any CVV. On the test bank page, enter any OTP of 4 to 10 digits.

Collect an invoice. A UPI Autopay debit is marked paid when Razorpay reports it settled, which can
take a while. **Logs** shows the call to Razorpay and Razorpay's answer.

## Go live

1. In the Razorpay Dashboard, switch to **Live** mode and generate the **live** keys
   (`rzp_live_…`).
2. If you offer cards, make sure Razorpay has enabled recurring card payments on the live
   account.
3. In your **live** business, add Razorpay with those keys.
4. Two Minute Billing registers a separate webhook for live mode. Check it under
   **Account & Settings → Webhooks** (under **Website and app settings**) in Razorpay.

## Troubleshoot

- **Check credentials says "We could not check the credentials." with "RazorPay auth/order
  failed: 401".** The key ID or the key secret is wrong, or they come from the other mode.
  Generate the keys again in the correct mode and update the gateway account.
- **Saving a payment method fails with "Razorpay requires a phone number for recurring
  payments."** Add a phone number to the customer, then try again.
- **A checkout or an invoice in a currency other than INR does not offer Razorpay.** Enable
  **International payments** on the gateway account. Do this only after Razorpay enables
  international payments on your Razorpay account.
- **A UPI Autopay debit stays pending for about a day.** Razorpay sends the customer a
  pre-debit notice and takes the money about 25 hours later. A netbanking debit can take up to
  2 working days. Two Minute Billing marks the invoice paid when Razorpay reports the debit.
- **A charge fails with "This charge of … is more than the … per-charge limit approved for this
  payment method."** The invoice is larger than the limit on the customer's mandate. Ask the
  customer to approve a new payment method. A retry cannot succeed.
