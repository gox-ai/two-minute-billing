Two Minute Billing charges cards on file through **your** Square account. You keep your Square
rates, and Square pays out to your bank as usual. When a customer pays an invoice once with **Pay
now**, Square also offers Google Pay, Cash App Pay, Afterpay and ACH. Apple Pay is coming soon.
These are not saved for renewals:
renewals charge the card on file.

## Before you start

- A Square account and an application in the Square Developer Console. The Sandbox works at once
  and comes with a test location.
- Square gives each application two sets of credentials: **Sandbox** and **Production**. Connect
  the Sandbox credentials to a test business and the Production credentials to a live business.
  Two Minute Billing refuses a mix.
- Each Square location charges in one currency: the currency of its country. Connect one gateway
  account for each location, and select that currency on it.
- You need permission to manage payment gateways in Two Minute Billing. The owner and admin
  roles have it.

## Find your API keys

In the Square Developer Console, open your application and select **Credentials** in the left
pane. At the top of the page, select **Sandbox** or **Production** to match the business you are
connecting.

You need three values:

| Field | Where to find it | Looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Access token** | Square Developer Console → your application → Credentials. Use the Sandbox access token for a test business and the Production access token for a live business. | — |
| **Application ID** | On the same page. A sandbox Application ID starts with sandbox-sq0idb-. | `sq0idp-…` |
| **Location ID** | Square Developer Console → your application → Locations. Each location charges in one currency: the currency of its country. | — |

::warning
**Keep the access token secret.** Anyone with the access token can charge and refund in your Square account. Paste it only into
Two Minute Billing, which stores it encrypted, and never into an email or a chat.
::

## Permissions

::warning
**Use the personal access token, not an OAuth token.** Copy the **access token** from your application's **Credentials** page. It has every permission
Two Minute Billing needs. An OAuth access token does not work: Square does not let an OAuth token
make webhook subscriptions, so the webhook cannot be registered.
::

With that token, Two Minute Billing uses these Square permissions:

| Square permission | Two Minute Billing uses it to |
| --- | --- |
| `PAYMENTS_WRITE`, `PAYMENTS_READ` | Charge, complete, cancel and refund payments, and save and disable cards |
| `CUSTOMERS_WRITE`, `CUSTOMERS_READ` | Make and find the customer in Square |
| `MERCHANT_PROFILE_READ` | Read the location and its currency |
| `ORDERS_WRITE`, `ORDERS_READ` | Make the Square checkout page for Apple Pay, and read its order when Square reports the payment |

**Webhook:** Two Minute Billing makes the webhook subscription with the same token and selects
the payment, refund, dispute and card events. The notification URL must use HTTPS.

## Connect it in Two Minute Billing

1. Open **Settings → Payment gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Select **Square** and paste the **Access token**, the **Application ID** and the
   **Location ID**.
3. Select the **currencies** and the **payment methods** this account handles.
4. Click **Create gateway account**.

Then open the account and click **Check credentials**. **Healthy** means Square accepted the
token, and the location is active and can take cards.

The payment methods you can turn on for Square:

<!-- generated:methods -->
- **Cards** — Apple Pay is coming soon.

### Apple Pay

**Coming soon.** Apple Pay on Square is not available yet. We did not complete its tests.

Apple Pay shows only when a customer pays an invoice now, in a browser that can pay with Apple
Pay (Safari with a card in Apple Wallet). The customer selects **Apple Pay** and pays on a Square
checkout page (a payment link), then comes back to the portal. Your domain does not need Apple
Pay verification: the page is on Square's domain. You do not change anything in Square or in Two
Minute Billing.

::note
**The Square checkout page also takes cards.** Square does not let a checkout page show Apple Pay
alone, and it does not tell Two Minute Billing whether a card or Apple Pay paid. So these
payments show as **Card** under **Paid with**. Square offers Apple Pay there only in the
countries where it supports Apple Pay. Apple Pay is not saved for renewals.
::

## Currencies

<!-- generated:currencies -->
Two Minute Billing offers these 6 currencies on Square:

AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, JPY, USD

Each Square location takes one currency: the currency of its country.

If you leave **Currencies** empty on the gateway account, the account takes each currency of your business that is on this list.

## Webhooks

Square tells Two Minute Billing about things that happen after a charge: a payment or a refund
that changes later, a refund made in Square, a dispute, a saved card that Square updates or
disables.

<!-- generated:webhooks -->
Two Minute Billing registers the webhook with Square for you when you save the account. Nothing to copy.

If the gateway account page shows the webhook as not configured:

1. Click “Register with Square” on the gateway account page. We make the webhook subscription and keep its signature key.
2. Square accepts only HTTPS notification URLs.
<!-- /generated:webhooks -->

## Test it

In your test business, create a customer and save the test card `4111 1111 1111 1111` with any
future expiry date and the CVV `111`. For USD, CAD and GBP, Square also needs a valid postal
code; do not use `99999`, which simulates an incorrect one. Collect an invoice: it is marked
paid, and **Logs** shows the call to Square and Square's answer.

The Square Sandbox does not show Apple Pay on checkout pages: it takes only test cards there. To
check the Apple Pay flow in a test business, use Safari, click **Pay now**, select **Apple Pay**
and pay on the Square page with the test card. The invoice is marked paid when Square's webhook
arrives.

## Go live

1. In the Square Developer Console, select **Production** on your application's
   **Credentials** page. Click **Show** and copy the **Production access token** and the
   **Application ID** (`sq0idp-…`).
2. Copy the ID of your production location under **Locations**.
3. In your **live** business, add Square with those values.
4. Two Minute Billing makes a separate webhook subscription for production. Check it under
   your application's **Webhooks → Subscriptions**.

## Troubleshoot

- **Check credentials says "square: a test business needs the Sandbox Application ID and access
  token".** The values come from Production. Copy the Sandbox ones, or connect them to a live
  business. A live business shows the opposite message.
- **Check credentials shows an error that starts with "Square get_location".** The access token
  or the location ID is wrong, or the location belongs to another application or environment.
  Copy both again from the same page and mode.
- **Check credentials says "We could not check the credentials." with the location status, for example "INACTIVE USD".** The
  location is not active or cannot take cards. Select an active location in Square.
- **Pay now shows no Apple Pay.** The customer's browser cannot pay with Apple Pay (it is not
  Safari, or Apple Wallet has no card). Apple Pay never shows when a customer saves a card.
- **The Square checkout page shows no Apple Pay button.** Square does not support Apple Pay in
  the location's country, or the page is in the Sandbox. The customer can pay with a card on the
  same page.
- **The account page shows the webhook as not configured.** Follow the steps under
  [Webhooks](#webhooks).
