Two Minute Billing charges cards and bank debits through **your** Stripe account. You keep your
Stripe rates, and Stripe pays out to your bank as usual.

## Before you start

- A Stripe account. Test mode works at once; live mode needs Stripe to activate your account.
- Stripe gives you two sets of keys: **test** and **live**. Connect the test keys to a test
  business and the live keys to a live business.
- You need permission to manage payment gateways in Two Minute Billing. The owner and admin
  roles have it.

## Find your API keys

In Stripe, open **Developers → API keys**. Make sure the **Test mode** toggle matches the business
you are connecting.

You need two values:

<!-- generated:keys -->
| Field | Where to find it | Looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Secret Key** | Stripe Dashboard → Developers → API keys. | `sk_live_…` |
| **Publishable Key** | On the same page. | `pk_live_…` |
<!-- /generated:keys -->

::warning
**Keep the secret key secret.** Anyone with the secret key can charge and refund in your Stripe account. Paste it only into
Two Minute Billing, which stores it encrypted, and never into an email or a chat.
::

## Permissions

::warning
**Use a key with every permission below.** The standard **secret key** has every permission. If you use a **restricted key** instead, give
it each permission in this table. A missing permission does not show when you connect: it shows
later, when a charge, a refund or the webhook fails.
::

| Stripe permission | Access | Two Minute Billing uses it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment Intents | Write | Charge, capture, cancel and find payments |
| Setup Intents | Write | Save a card or a bank mandate |
| Customers | Write | Make and update the customer in Stripe |
| Payment Methods | Write | Attach and remove saved payment methods |
| Charges and Refunds | Write | Refund payments and read refunds made in Stripe |
| Payment Disputes | Read | Read a dispute when Stripe reports it |
| Mandates | Read | Read the mandate of a bank debit |
| Balance | Read | Check the credentials |
| Accounts | Read | Read your account's country when a payment method is saved |
| Payment Method Domains | Write | Register your payment domain for Apple Pay |
| Webhook Endpoints | Write | Register the webhook for you |

## Connect it in Two Minute Billing

1. Open **Settings → Payment gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Select **Stripe** and paste the **Secret Key** and the **Publishable Key**.
3. Select the **currencies** and the **payment methods** this account handles.
4. Click **Create gateway account**.

Then open the account and click **Check credentials**. **Healthy** means Stripe accepted the key.

The payment methods you can turn on for Stripe:

<!-- generated:methods -->
- **Cards**
- **Direct Debit (ACH)** — US only. The debit settles asynchronously.
- **Direct Debit (SEPA)** — EU only. The debit settles asynchronously.
<!-- /generated:methods -->

## Webhooks

Stripe tells Two Minute Billing about things that happen after a charge: a bank debit that
settles days later, a refund made in the Stripe dashboard, a dispute.

<!-- generated:webhooks -->
Two Minute Billing registers the webhook with Stripe for you when you save the account. Nothing to copy.

If the gateway account page shows the webhook as not configured:

1. Click “Register with Stripe” on the gateway account page. We create the endpoint and keep the signing secret.
2. Optional: confirm it appears under Developers → Webhooks in Stripe.
<!-- /generated:webhooks -->

## Test it

In your test business, create a customer and save the test card `4242 4242 4242 4242` with any
future expiry date and any CVC. Collect an invoice: it is marked paid, and **Logs** shows the
call to Stripe and Stripe's answer.

## Go live

1. In Stripe, turn **Test mode** off and copy the **live** keys (`sk_live_…`, `pk_live_…`).
2. In your **live** business, add Stripe with those keys.
3. Two Minute Billing registers a separate webhook for live mode. Check it under
   **Developers → Webhooks** in Stripe.

## Troubleshoot

- **Check credentials says "We could not check the credentials." with "Invalid API Key
  provided".** The secret key is wrong or was rolled in Stripe. Copy it again from
  **Developers → API keys** and update the gateway account.
- **The account page shows the webhook as not configured.** Follow the steps under
  [Webhooks](#webhooks).
- **A bank debit stays pending.** ACH and SEPA debits take several business days to settle.
  Two Minute Billing marks the invoice paid when Stripe reports the debit settled.
