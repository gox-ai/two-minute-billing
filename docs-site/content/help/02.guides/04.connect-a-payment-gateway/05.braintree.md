Two Minute Billing charges saved cards, PayPal accounts and Venmo accounts through **your**
Braintree account. You keep your Braintree rates, and Braintree pays out to your bank as usual.

::note
**Braintree does not offer Apple Pay here.** Apple Pay on the web needs the payment page's domain
verified with Braintree's own file. The customer portal is on one shared domain, which already
holds another gateway's file, and Braintree has no payment page of its own on its domain. To take
Apple Pay, connect a gateway whose guide has an Apple Pay section.
::

## Before you start

- A Braintree sandbox account to test with, and a production account to go live.
- Braintree has a separate **sandbox** and **production** Control Panel, with different API keys
  and a different merchant ID. Connect the sandbox values to a test business and the production
  values to a live business.
- Each Braintree merchant account settles in one currency. Find the merchant account ID for each
  currency you sell in.
- You need permission to manage payment gateways in Two Minute Billing. The owner and admin
  roles have it.
- **PayPal** (optional): your PayPal business account must be linked to Braintree. See
  [Turn on PayPal, Venmo, 3D Secure and the account updater](#turn-on-paypal-venmo-3d-secure-and-the-account-updater).
- **Venmo** (optional): only for a business in the United States that charges in US dollars.
  Braintree turns it on for your merchant account.
- **3D Secure** (optional): if it is on in your Braintree account, buyers confirm their card with
  their bank when they save it or pay now. Renewals do not ask the buyer again.

## Find your API keys

Sign in to the Braintree Control Panel for the environment you are connecting: sandbox or
production. Click the gear icon at the top right and select **API**. Under **API Keys**, click
**View** in the **Private Key** column. If there is no key, click **Generate New API Key**.

The **Merchant ID** is on the same API page. For the merchant account IDs, click the gear icon and
select **Business**: they are in the first column under **Merchant Accounts**.

You need these values:

<!-- generated:keys -->
| Field | Where to find it | Looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Merchant ID** | Braintree Control Panel → Settings → API. | — |
| **Public Key** | On the same page, under API Keys. | — |
| **Private Key** | On the same page. Click View in the Private Key column. | — |
| **Currencies and merchant accounts** | Optional. Each Braintree merchant account settles in one currency. Type the currency of your default merchant account, for example EUR. To charge in more currencies, add CURRENCY=merchant account ID for each one, separated by commas. If you leave this field empty, we charge in USD on your default merchant account. | `USD=fernhill_usd, EUR=fernhill_eur` |
<!-- /generated:keys -->

For example, `USD=fernhill_usd, EUR=fernhill_eur` charges US dollars on one merchant account
and euros on the other. Select the same currencies on the gateway account.

::warning
**Keep the private key secret.** Anyone with the public and the private key can charge and refund in your Braintree account.
Paste them only into Two Minute Billing, which stores them encrypted, and never into an email or
a chat.
::

## Permissions

::warning
**The API keys have the permissions of the user who made them.** Braintree API keys belong to a Control Panel user, and they can do only what that user's role
allows. Make the keys as a user with the **Account Admin** role, or with a custom role that has
every permission below. If the user is disabled or the role changes, the keys stop working.
::

| Braintree role permission | Two Minute Billing uses it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Sales** | Charge saved cards |
| **Submit Sales for Settlement** | Capture an authorized payment |
| **Void Transactions** | Cancel a payment before it settles |
| **Credit Previous Transactions (Refunds)** | Refund payments |
| **Manage Customers and Payment Methods** | Make customers and save and remove cards |
| **View Transactions** and **View Payment Methods** | Read payments and saved cards |
| **Search Transactions** and **Download Transactions with Masked Payment Data** | Find refunds made in Braintree, and payments whose answer was lost on the way |

The user must also have access to every merchant account you enter under **Currencies and
merchant accounts**.

**Webhook:** you add the webhook by hand. The user who adds it needs the **Manage Webhooks**
permission.

PayPal, Venmo, 3D Secure and the account updater use the same permissions. They need no other
role permission: you turn them on for your Braintree account instead.

### Turn on PayPal, Venmo, 3D Secure and the account updater

| Feature | What to do in Braintree | What happens if it is off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PayPal** | In the Control Panel, go to **Settings → Account Settings → Payment Methods → PayPal**. Link your PayPal business account (in the sandbox: **Link Sandbox**, with a PayPal sandbox business account). | Buyers do not see the PayPal button. |
| **Venmo** | Ask Braintree to turn on Venmo for your US dollar merchant account. | Buyers do not see the Venmo button. A Venmo account saved before fails at the first charge. |
| **3D Secure** | Ask Braintree to turn it on, then set your rules under **Settings → Fraud Management → 3D Secure Rules**. | Cards are saved and charged without 3D Secure. |
| **Account updater** | Ask Braintree to turn it on. It is for businesses in the US or that sell mostly to US buyers, and for Visa, Mastercard and Discover cards. | New card numbers and expiry dates do not come in. |

::warning
**Let PayPal accept the same invoice number twice.** Two Minute Billing sends the invoice number
with each PayPal payment, and a retry of a failed renewal sends the same number again. If your
PayPal account blocks duplicate invoice IDs, PayPal refuses every retry. In PayPal, open **Account
Settings → Website payments → Block accidental payments** and select **No, allow multiple payments
per invoice ID**.
::

If a buyer cannot complete 3D Secure, Two Minute Billing does not save the card or take the
payment. It follows Braintree's own list of 3D Secure results to refuse. A card that cannot use
3D Secure at all (for example, the bank does not take part) is saved and charged as usual.

## Connect it in Two Minute Billing

1. Open **Settings → Payment gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Select **Braintree** and paste the **Merchant ID**, the **Public Key** and the
   **Private Key**. Type your currencies and merchant accounts.
3. Select the **currencies** and the **payment methods** this account handles.
4. Click **Create gateway account**.

Then open the account and click **Check credentials**. **Healthy** means Braintree accepted the
keys. The check does not test the merchant accounts: a wrong merchant account ID shows at the
first charge in that currency. It does not test PayPal or Venmo either: if a method is not on in
Braintree, buyers do not see its button.

The payment methods you can turn on for Braintree:

<!-- generated:methods -->
- **Cards** — If 3D Secure is on in your Braintree account, the buyer confirms the card with the bank.
- **PayPal** — Link your PayPal business account in the Braintree Control Panel first.
- **Venmo** — US businesses only, USD only. Ask Braintree to turn on Venmo for your merchant account first.
<!-- /generated:methods -->

## Webhooks

Braintree tells Two Minute Billing about disputes, about payments that settle or fail to
settle, about PayPal and Venmo accounts that a buyer disconnects, and about cards that the account
updater changes.

<!-- generated:webhooks -->
Braintree does not let Two Minute Billing register the webhook, so you add it in Braintree yourself.

1. In the Braintree Control Panel, go to Settings → Webhooks → Create New Webhook.
2. Paste the notification URL from the gateway account page.
3. Select all Disputes events, the Payment Method Revoked By Customer event and the Account Updater Daily Report event.
4. If the list shows Refund Failed, select it.
5. If the list shows Transaction Settled and Transaction Settlement Declined, select them. The list shows them only when ACH or SEPA Direct Debit is enabled on your merchant account.
6. Do not select subscription events. Then click Create Webhook.
7. Your API keys sign the events. You do not need a webhook secret.
<!-- /generated:webhooks -->

A refund made in the Braintree Control Panel, or a payment declined at settlement, does not
come by webhook. Two Minute Billing finds it by a search each hour, so it can take up to an hour
to show.

When a buyer disconnects PayPal or Venmo from your business, the saved method is marked
**invalid** and the next renewal uses the customer's backup method. When the account updater
changes a card's number or expiry date, the saved card shows the new last four digits and expiry
date. The card keeps the same Braintree token.

## Test it

In your test business, create a customer and save the test card `4111 1111 1111 1111` with a
future expiry date. Collect an invoice: it is marked paid, and **Logs** shows the call to
Braintree and Braintree's answer.

To see a decline, charge an amount from 2000.00 to 2999.99: the sandbox declines it.

**PayPal.** The PayPal button in the payment form needs a linked PayPal sandbox account (see
[Turn on PayPal](#turn-on-paypal-venmo-3d-secure-and-the-account-updater)). Sign in with a PayPal
sandbox **personal** account, not your business account: PayPal declines a payment from your own
business account. A PayPal payment settles at once, so you can refund part of it straight away.

**Venmo.** Your sandbox merchant account needs Venmo turned on. Without it, a Venmo charge fails
as a payment gateway setup problem, and **Logs** shows Braintree's answer "Merchant account does
not support payment instrument".

**3D Secure.** With 3D Secure on in your sandbox, save these cards with an expiry date in January,
three years from now:

| Card number | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `4000 0000 0000 2701` | Confirmed without a challenge. The card is saved. |
| `4000 0000 0000 2503` | Confirmed after a challenge. The card is saved. |
| `4000 0000 0000 2370` | The challenge fails. The card is not saved. |
| `4000 0000 0000 2925` | The bank refuses without a challenge. The card is not saved. |
| `4000 0000 0000 2990` | 3D Secure is not available. The card is saved without it. |

The account updater cannot be tested in the sandbox.

## Go live

1. Sign in to the **production** Control Panel and copy the production **Merchant ID**,
   **Public Key**, **Private Key** and merchant account IDs.
2. In your **live** business, add Braintree with those values.
3. Add the webhook in the production Control Panel too, with the notification URL of the live
   gateway account. Follow the steps under [Webhooks](#webhooks).
4. Link your PayPal business account in the production Control Panel, and ask Braintree to turn
   on Venmo, 3D Secure or the account updater there if you use them.

## Troubleshoot

- **Check credentials says "We could not check the credentials." with "Braintree refused the
  API keys (HTTP 401)".** The keys are wrong, or they come from the other environment. Copy them
  again from the Control Panel that matches the business: sandbox for test, production for live.
- **A charge fails with "This Braintree account has no merchant account for EUR."** Add that
  currency to **Currencies and merchant accounts**, for example `EUR=lumenfield_eur`. Or connect
  a gateway that takes that currency.
- **The payment form shows no PayPal or Venmo button.** The method is not on in Braintree for
  this merchant account, or not selected on the gateway account. Venmo also shows only for US
  dollars and in a browser that Venmo supports.
- **"Your bank did not confirm this card."** 3D Secure failed or the buyer closed the challenge.
  The buyer can try again or use a different card. Nothing was charged.
- **Check credentials shows "does not start with a currency code".** Each item in
  **Currencies and merchant accounts** starts with a three-letter currency code, such as `USD` or
  `USD=lumenfield_usd`. Separate the items with commas.
- **Pay now shows no Apple Pay.** That is expected: Braintree does not offer Apple Pay here (see
  the note at the top of this page).
