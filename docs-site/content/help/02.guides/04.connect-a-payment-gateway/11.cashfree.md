Two Minute Billing collects recurring payments through **your** Cashfree account: UPI Autopay,
eNACH and card mandates in rupees, and card mandates in USD and EUR. The customer approves a
mandate once, and Two Minute Billing debits each renewal against it. Invoices paid now can also
be in USD, EUR, GBP, AED or JPY, by card. You keep your Cashfree rates, and Cashfree settles to your bank as usual.

## Before you start

- A Cashfree account. Test keys are available at once. Production keys need Cashfree to activate
  your account, and two-factor authentication to show them. Connect the test keys to a test
  business and the production keys to a live business.
- UPI Autopay and eNACH mandates are INR only. Card mandates take INR, USD and EUR. Pay now
  also takes cards in USD, EUR, GBP, AED and JPY. Cashfree enables each currency for each
  account: ask Cashfree to enable international payments for the currencies you sell in. Then
  enable **International payments** on the gateway account in Two Minute Billing.
  Give a phone number outside India with `+` and the country code (for example
  `+14155550100`).
- Two Minute Billing asks Cashfree for at least 1 whole unit of the currency (for example
  1 USD or 1 JPY) on each payment.
- Each customer who pays by mandate needs a **name** and a **phone number** in Two Minute
  Billing. Cashfree refuses a mandate without them. An INR mandate needs a 10-digit Indian
  mobile number.
- You need permission to manage payment gateways in Two Minute Billing. The owner and admin
  roles have it.

## Find your API keys

In the Cashfree Merchant Dashboard, open **Developers → API Keys**. For test keys, click
**Switch to Test** at the top right first. To see a full key, click the icon next to it and
select **View API Key**.

**Keep the Secret Key secret.** Anyone with the App ID and the Secret Key can collect and refund in your Cashfree account. Paste

chat.

## Permissions

::warning
**Cashfree keys have no permission settings.** A Cashfree App ID and Secret Key can use every product that is active on your account, so there
is nothing to select on the key. Check the items below instead: a missing one does not show when
you connect, only later when a mandate or a payment fails.
::

- **Subscriptions.** Mandates need the Cashfree **Subscriptions** product on your account. If a
  mandate cannot be made, ask Cashfree to activate it.
- **Domain whitelisting (live only).** In production, Cashfree accepts payments only from
  approved domains. Add the domain where your customers pay under **Developers → Whitelisting**.
  Cashfree reviews it in about 24 hours.
- **Webhook versions.** The **Payments** endpoint must use version **2025-01-01** and the
  **Subscriptions** endpoint version **2026-01-01**. **Check credentials** tells you if a version
  is wrong.

## Connect it in Two Minute Billing

1. Open **Settings → Payment gateways** and click **Add gateway**.
2. Select **Cashfree** and paste the **App ID** and the **Secret Key**.
3. Select the **currencies** and the **payment methods** this account handles.
4. Click **Create gateway account**.

Then open the account and click **Check credentials**. **Healthy** means Cashfree accepted the
keys. After the first webhooks arrive, the check also tells you if a webhook is on the wrong
version.

The payment methods you can turn on for Cashfree:

<!-- generated:methods -->
- **Cards** — Saved cards: INR, USD and EUR. Cashfree sends the debit notice for INR. We send it for USD and EUR. Pay now also takes GBP, AED and JPY. Cashfree must enable international payments on your account.
- **UPI Autopay** — INR only. The debit settles asynchronously.
- **eNACH** — INR only. The bank approves the mandate in 1 to 2 days.
<!-- /generated:methods -->

For UPI Autopay, Two Minute Billing asks Cashfree to send the customer a pre-debit notice, then
takes the debit about 25 hours after Cashfree delivers it. For eNACH and INR cards, Cashfree
sends the notice itself. For USD and EUR cards, Two Minute Billing emails the notice to the
customer.

## Currencies

<!-- generated:currencies -->
Two Minute Billing offers these 6 currencies on Cashfree:

AED, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, USD

Currencies other than INR need Cashfree to enable international payments on your account. Cashfree settles in INR.

A new gateway account takes INR only. After Cashfree enables international payments on your account, enable **International payments** when you create the gateway account in Two Minute Billing, or later in its settings. Until you do, Cashfree gets no payments in other currencies.

Some payment methods take one currency only:

| Payment method | Currency |
| --- | --- |
| UPI Autopay | INR |
| eNACH | INR |

To save a payment method, Cashfree takes INR, USD and EUR only.

If you leave **Currencies** empty on the gateway account, the account takes each currency of your business that is on this list.
<!-- /generated:currencies -->

## Webhooks

Cashfree tells Two Minute Billing about everything that happens after a mandate is set up: a debit
that succeeds or fails, a mandate the customer cancels, a refund, a dispute. Payment events and
subscription events come from two different tabs on Cashfree's webhook page, so you add the same
address twice: on the **Payments** tab with version **2025-01-01**, and on the **Subscriptions**
tab with version **2026-01-01**.

<!-- generated:webhooks -->
Cashfree does not let Two Minute Billing register the webhook, so you add it in Cashfree yourself.

1. In Cashfree: go to Developers → Webhooks → Add Webhook Endpoint.
2. Paste the notification URL from the gateway account page.
3. Select webhook version 2025-01-01. Select these events: success payment, failed payment, user dropped payment, instrument active, instrument failed, refund, auto refund, dispute created, dispute updated and dispute closed.
4. Click Continue and save. Then copy the webhook endpoint's Secret Key (not your API Secret Key), paste it on the gateway account page and click Save.
5. On the Subscriptions tab of the same page, add the same URL. Select webhook version 2026-01-01. Then select all subscription events.
<!-- /generated:webhooks -->

In Cashfree's webhook form, you can click **Test** to send a test event to the address. It
succeeds when the address is correct.

## Test it

In your test business, create a customer with a name and a 10-digit mobile number, and set up a
UPI Autopay mandate for it. When Cashfree's page asks for a UPI ID, enter the test UPI ID
`testsuccess@gocash` (`testfailure@gocash` makes it fail). Collect an invoice: Two Minute Billing asks Cashfree to send the
pre-debit notice, and the invoice is marked paid after the debit about a day later. **Logs** shows each
call to Cashfree and Cashfree's answer.

## Go live

1. In Cashfree, turn off test mode and open **Developers → API Keys**. Copy the production
   **App ID** and **Secret Key**.
2. If you sell in USD, EUR, GBP, AED or JPY, ask Cashfree to enable each of these currencies on
   your production account. Cashfree has no API that shows them, so Check credentials cannot
   find a currency that is not enabled. A payment in that currency then fails at Cashfree.
3. In your **live** business, add Cashfree with those keys.
4. Add both webhooks in production with the same steps as under [Webhooks](#webhooks), and paste
   each webhook endpoint's Secret Key (not your API Secret Key) on the live gateway account page. Production webhook addresses must
   use `https`.

## Troubleshoot

- **Check credentials says "We could not check the credentials." with "authentication
  failed".** The App ID or the Secret Key is wrong, or they belong to the other environment.
  Copy both again from **Developers → API Keys**, with test mode set to match the business.
- **Check credentials says "Correct the settings below." with "Cashfree sends … webhooks on
  version …".** Select the version that the message names on that tab in Cashfree:
  **2025-01-01** on the Payments tab, **2026-01-01** on the Subscriptions tab.
- **A mandate setup is refused with "Cashfree requires a phone number for recurring payments" or
  "Cashfree requires the customer's name for recurring payments".** Add the missing detail to
  the customer, then try again. For an INR mandate, the phone number must be a 10-digit Indian
  mobile number.
- **A checkout or an invoice in USD, EUR, GBP, AED or JPY does not offer Cashfree.** Enable
  **International payments** on the gateway account. Do this only after Cashfree enables
  international payments on your Cashfree account.
- **A card mandate in USD or EUR is refused with "A bank in India issued this card".** Cashfree does
  not take subscription payments in another currency from a card issued in India. Ask the
  customer to use a card issued outside India, or to subscribe in INR.
- **A charge fails with "The customer authorized this payment method for payments in INR
  only".** The
  invoice is in another currency than the customer's mandate. Two Minute Billing does not send
  the charge to Cashfree. Ask the customer to add a payment method for that currency.
- **A UPI renewal is not paid on the day it is due.** This is expected. Cashfree must deliver the
  pre-debit notice first, and the debit follows about 25 hours later.
