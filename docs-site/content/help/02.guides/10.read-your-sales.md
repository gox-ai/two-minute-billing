**Reports → Sales** is for the one-time products you sell: a template, a license, a credit pack
bought once, a single service. It shows how much you sold, who bought, what came back and where
the orders come from.

The **Sales** entry shows in the **Reports** menu only when the business has one-time sales in the
last 90 days. If you open the page by link before that, it says there are no one-time sales and
shows data when there are some. See [Read your numbers](/help/guides/read-your-numbers#reports-follow-your-billing-models).

## Choose the period

At the top of the page, select the date range and the **Currency**. Each figure compares with the
period before it, unless you turn the comparison off. Money is shown in one currency at a time.
Two Minute Billing never adds amounts in different currencies.

## The headline figures

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | Invoices with no subscription and at least one one-time line, raised in the period. Credit packs are not orders |
| **Order revenue** | What the orders are worth, before tax, less discounts and promotional credit. |
| **Average order value** | Order revenue divided by orders |
| **Buyers** | Customers who placed an order |
| **Returning buyer rate** | The share of buyers who had ordered before |
| **Refunds** | Money returned in the period on payments for orders, tax included |
| **Refund rate** | Refunds as a share of order revenue. Both are counted before tax, so a fully refunded order reads 100% with or without tax |
| **Revenue per buyer, all time** | Order revenue divided by buyers, up to the end of the period |

Subscription payments and credit packs are not orders. Subscription payments show in the
subscription reports and in the **Revenue** dashboard. Credit packs show in
**Reports → Credits**.

## Where the orders come from

- **Orders and revenue**: order revenue per day, with the orders as markers.
- **By product**: units, revenue and refunds for each product, largest revenue first. Select a
  product name to open your product list.
- **First-time and returning buyers**: order revenue from each group.
- **Discounts**: what you gave away on orders. Select **Repeat buyers** to open the customers
  with two or more orders.
- **Order revenue by country**, **by payment gateway** and **by coupon**.

## See every order

Select **Orders** in the sidebar to see each order as a row. The **Orders, 30 days** card on Home
and the **Orders** figure on this page open the same list. A row shows the order number, the customer, the
products, the amount, the status, the date, the payment method and the coupon. Use the column menu
to add the customer email, the billing country, the discount, the tax, the refunded amount, the gateway
and more. Select a row to open the order.

The order page shows the items with their totals, the payments, the refunds, the customer, the
coupon and the documents. Use it to download the invoice, send an email or refund the order.

The status is **Paid**, **Due**, **Refunded** or **Partly refunded**. Use the tabs to see one status.
You can filter by status, date, customer, country, product, coupon, gateway, payment method,
amount, refunded amount and the custom fields of the customer, and save a view. Credit pack
purchases are not orders. They are in **Invoices**.

## Do buyers come back

Two tables group buyers by the month of their first order:

- **Repeat purchases by cohort**: the share of each month's first-time buyers who ordered again.
- **Revenue per buyer by cohort**: the order revenue so far, divided by the buyers in the cohort.

A table shows only when the business has enough orders to fill it.
