Billy is the assistant inside the dashboard. Ask it about your invoices, customers, subscriptions, payments and MRR, or about how Two Minute Billing works. Billy reads your data through the same API as the dashboard, checks every figure in its answer against that data, and links to the screen where you act.

## Open Billy

- Select the round Billy button at the bottom right of the screen, or press **⌘J** (**Ctrl+J** on Windows and Linux).
- On an invoice, customer, subscription or payment page, open Billy. Billy then answers about that record. The first time you see each type of record, a card above the Billy button shows **Ask about this invoice** (or customer, subscription, payment).
- In the search box (**⌘K**), type a question and select **Ask Billy**.

Billy shows questions to start with for the page you are on. Your chats are your own: no other member sees a chat unless you share it. A shared chat can be read by the members of your organization. They cannot reply.

## What an answer shows

- **The steps Billy took**, such as "Explained invoice INV-1043".
- **The answer**, with record cards and small tables. Select a record to open it.
- **"Every figure found in your data"** when Billy found each number in what it read. A number that Billy could not find has a dotted underline and a warning. Check it before you use it.
- **Questions to ask next.** Select one to send it.

Under each answer, select **Copy**, **Ask again**, or rate the answer as **Helpful** or **Not helpful**. When an answer is not helpful, you can add a short comment. We read these comments to make Billy better.

## When Billy asks a question

Some requests need a choice, for example which of two customers you mean, or how to treat unused time when a subscription is cancelled. Billy then asks one short question with buttons. Your business's own default is selected and labelled **Your default**. Select an option, press its number key, or select **Type a different answer**.

## Send a request to our support team

On Two Minute Billing Cloud, Billy answers first. When you ask for a person, when Billy cannot answer, or when a request fails and Billy cannot explain why, Billy offers to send the details to our support team. Billy never sends a request: it shows a card, and you decide.

1. Read the card. Select **Edit** to change the subject or the text.
2. Look at the items that Billy attached: the page, the records, an error, the conversation and your browser. Select **×** on an item to remove it. To add an image, select **Attach a screenshot**.
3. Select **Send to support**. The card shows the reference of your request, for example **SR-1042**.

We reply in Billy and by email. A dot on the Billy button shows a reply that you did not read. To see your requests and reply to us, open Billy and select the support button at the top of the panel. A reply opens a resolved request again.

A request contains only what you can see in the dashboard. Billy removes API keys, passwords and card numbers from the text before it goes to us.

## Tools in the message box

Select **+** in the message box to:

- **Attach a screenshot**, such as a bank's email or an error from another site, for Billy to explain. Billy accepts images only: PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. You can also paste an image.
- **Mention a record**: type **@** and a name, an email or an invoice number to point Billy at a customer, invoice or subscription.
- **Start a task**: explain an invoice, explain an MRR change, find a screen, or search the help.

Screenshots stay with the conversation. We delete them when you delete the conversation, and in all cases after 90 days.

## Billy uses your permissions

Billy can read and do only what your role allows. Each look-up goes through the API with your sign-in, so a member who cannot see invoices gets no answers about invoices. See [Your team, roles and logs](/help/guides/your-team).

## Changes that you confirm

An owner can let Billy prepare changes. Then you can ask, for example, "pause dunning on this invoice until Friday" or "move this customer to the yearly plan".

1. Billy reads the record, asks for any choice it needs, and gets the engine's preview where there is one, for example what a cancellation does to money.
2. Billy shows a card with exactly what it will send and the preview figures. For an email, the card shows the email as your customer will see it, with the recipient and the subject. You can change the note that Billy wrote, and the email shows your change. Nothing changes yet.
3. Select **Confirm** to make the change, or **Back** to stop. A card that you do not confirm expires after 15 minutes.

The change is made with your permissions, one time only. When you ask for a customer portal link, the card shows the link one time after you confirm. Billy does not see it, and we do not keep it. The record's activity log shows that you made it, **via Billy**.

**Money changes** are refunds, credit notes, collecting a payment now, charging a payment request, invoicing now, applying credits, recording a payment or a refund, voiding or writing off an invoice, finalizing or regenerating an invoice, voiding a credit note, changing promotional credit, topping up a wallet, and cancelling now with a credit or a refund. They need a second switch and, each time, a second confirmation: "You cannot undo this".

Billy never deletes records, never changes payment methods, settings, the team or API keys, and never makes a change through an AI tool that you connect from outside (see [AI assistants (MCP)](/help/developers/getting-started/connect-ai-assistants)).

### Let Billy make changes

An owner or admin goes to **Settings → Billy**:

- **Let Billy take actions**: Billy can prepare changes. It is off at first.
- **Allow money actions**: also money changes. You can turn it on only when actions are on.

## Included use and your own key

On Two Minute Billing Cloud, each plan includes Billy credits every month: 50 on Free, 300 on Launch, 1,000 on Growth and 3,000 on Scale. A credit is about one question. A long question that reads a lot of data can use more than one. **Settings → Billy** shows how many credits you used this month. Credits start again on the first day of each month.

To use Billy beyond your credits, add your own key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google in **Settings → Billy**. Questions then go to your provider on your account, and they do not count against your credits. We store the key encrypted and show only its last four characters.

An owner can turn Billy off for the organization in **Settings → Billy**.

## Where your data goes

To answer, Billy sends your question and the data it read for it to the AI model: our model provider on Cloud, or your provider when you add your own key. Customer names, notes and metadata are sent as data, and Billy treats them as data, never as instructions. Screenshots are sent as images.

## Self-hosted installations

Billy is off until you set a model in the engine's environment: `ASSISTANT_PROVIDER` (`openai`, `anthropic` or `google`), `ASSISTANT_MODEL` and `ASSISTANT_API_KEY`. To use a local model with an OpenAI-compatible server, also set `ASSISTANT_BASE_URL`. There are no credits on a self-hosted installation, and no limit unless you set `ASSISTANT_ORG_MONTHLY_TOKENS`.
