This recipe continues [Bill AI tokens](/help/developers/recipes/bill-ai-tokens). There, usage
bills on the invoice at the end of the period. Here, the customer pays first: they buy credit,
your app checks the credit before a model call, and the usage that the credit covers costs them
nothing more on the invoice.

## How credit works

A **credit grant** gives one customer an amount of credit. The credit is money, or an amount of
your own **credit unit**, for example 1 credit = USD 0.01.

| | Promotional | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| The customer pays | Nothing | An invoice for the credit |
| Starts | At once | When the invoice is paid |
| Cost basis | 0 | The net price on the invoice |
| Applies | Before tax | Before tax if taxed at purchase, after tax if taxed at use |
| Ends early | Void | A credit note on its invoice |

When an invoice is finalised, grants pay its usage lines in this order: restored credit first,
then the lowest `priority`, then the soonest expiry, promotional before paid, then the oldest.
Usage that no grant covers bills as usual.

## Create a credit unit

Skip this step to sell credit in money. A unit lets you show "1,000 credits" instead of
"USD 10.00". `minor_per_unit` is in minor units, so `"1"` is one cent:

```bash [curl]
curl -X POST $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/credit_units \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "credit",
    "name": "credit",
    "plural_name": "credits",
    "currency": "USD",
    "minor_per_unit": "1",
    "decimals": 0
  }'
```

The first grant in the unit fixes `currency`, `minor_per_unit` and `decimals`. After that you can
change only the names. In the dashboard: **Settings → Credit units**.

### The money value of a credit

`minor_per_unit` is what one credit is worth. We use it in two places:

- **Usage.** A usage line is priced in money, and a grant pays it in credits at this rate. Give the
  meter's price the same rate (`unit_amount_decimal`) as the unit, so one credit of usage uses one
  credit. With a unit of `"60"` and a price of `"60"`, an action that sends 1 to the meter uses 1
  credit.
- **Bookkeeping.** The face value of a grant (credits × `minor_per_unit`) is what the ledger
  records: the cost of promotional credit, the revenue when paid credit is used, and the breakage
  when paid credit expires unused.

An app that counts "1 credit = 1 image" and sells packs at different prices does not need a money
value: create the unit with `minor_per_unit: null` and give each meter a credit cost. See
[Credits in your own unit](/help/developers/recipes/credits-in-your-own-unit).

### Fractions of a credit

`decimals` (0 to 4) is how finely a credit divides. Every `amount` in that unit (grants, packs,
plan credits) is a whole number of the smallest step, 10^-`decimals` credits. For an action that
costs half a credit, use `decimals: 1`; then 25 credits is `"amount": 250`, and the access check's
`available_units[].units` is in tenths too.

## Sell credit packs

A credit pack is a one-time price with `grants_credit`. The buyer pays through checkout, a payment
link, the portal's **Buy credits** list or an invoice that you create. Each pack line on a paid
invoice gives one paid grant:

```bash [curl]
curl -X POST $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/prices \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "product": "ai-credits",
    "name": "1,000 credits",
    "type": "one_time",
    "model": "flat",
    "currency": "USD",
    "amount_minor": 900,
    "grants_credit": {
      "kind": "pack",
      "unit": "credit",
      "amount": 1000,
      "category": "paid",
      "expires_after": { "days": 365 },
      "tax_treatment": "taxed_at_purchase"
    }
  }'
```

This pack sells 1,000 credits (face value USD 10.00) for USD 9.00. The cost basis is USD 9.00,
and USD 1.00 is a discount that the ledger records when the credit is used.

`tax_treatment` decides when tax is charged:

- `taxed_at_purchase`: the pack invoice is taxed. Usage that the credit pays has no tax.
- `taxed_at_use`: the pack invoice has no tax. Usage is taxed, and the credit pays the tax too.
  Only for credit in money.

To set a default for all packs, set `credit_config.default_tax_treatment` on the business.

## Include credits in a plan

On a recurring price, `grants_credit` with `kind: "per_cycle"` gives promotional credit each
billing period. `rollover` decides what happens to credit that is not used:

```json
"grants_credit": {
  "kind": "per_cycle",
  "unit": "credit",
  "amount": 5000,
  "rollover": { "mode": "carry", "percent": 50, "cap": 2000, "expire_after_cycles": 1 }
}
```

At each renewal, half of the unused credit (at most 2,000) moves to a new grant that lasts one
more period. Carried credit does not move a second time. `"mode": "none"` lets unused credit
expire, and `"expire_after_cycles": null` keeps carried credit until it is used.

The grant is `amount` × the item's quantity. Other fields:

| Field | Values | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `reset` | `{ "every": "month" }`; `every` is `hour`, `day`, `week`, `month`, `quarter`, `half_year`, `year`, `term` or `never`; optional `count` (every 2 months) and `anchor` (`subscription`, the default, or `calendar`) | Each window gets its own grant, and the last window's credit ends. Omitted or `term`: one grant per billing period. A window cannot be longer than the billing period. |
| `first_window` | `full`, `prorated`, `next_full_window` | A calendar window that started before the subscription: grant it in full, prorated, or not at all. Omitted: the business setting (default `full`). |
| `on_exhausted` | `block`, `bill` | What usage does when this plan's credit is used up, when the customer and the grants set nothing. Needs blocking on (below). |

Monthly credits on a yearly plan: put `"reset": { "every": "month" }` on the yearly price. The
customer pays once a year and gets `amount` each month; unused credit ends at each month end
unless `rollover` carries it.

```json
"grants_credit": {
  "kind": "per_cycle",
  "unit": "credit",
  "amount": 250,
  "reset": { "every": "month" },
  "rollover": { "mode": "none" },
  "on_exhausted": "block"
}
```

A trial gets no plan credits: the first grant comes when the trial ends and the first paid period
starts. To give credit during a trial, add a promotional grant by hand (below) that expires at
the trial end.

On a plan change, the business's plan credit rules decide what happens
(`billing_rules_config.plan_credits` on `PATCH /v1/businesses/{biz}`; in the dashboard,
**Settings → Billing rules**). `POST …/subscriptions/{sub}/change` takes `plan_credits` to choose
for one change.

| Rule | Values (default first) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `on_upgrade` | `full`, `prorated`, `next_window` | `full`: the old plan's grant ends and the new plan's grant comes at once, in full. `prorated`: the new grant is cut to the rest of the window. `next_window`: the old grant runs to its window end and the new plan grants from the next window. |
| `on_downgrade` | `next_window`, `full` | `next_window`: the old grant runs to its window end and the lower plan grants from the window after the one that holds the change. `full`: the old grant ends and the lower plan's grant comes at once. |
| `first_window` | `full`, `prorated`, `next_full_window` | The calendar window that started before the subscription (above). |
| `on_pause` | `skip`, `keep_granting`, `shift` | Windows during a pause. |

::warning
A downgrade at the end of the term with `on_downgrade: "next_window"` takes effect at the same
instant the new window starts. The old plan's grant ends there, and the lower plan's first grant
comes one window later, so the customer pays for the lower plan and has no plan credit for that
first window. For a product that blocks usage at zero credit, set `on_downgrade: "full"`, or send
`plan_credits: "full"` on that change.
::

## Give credit by hand

Promotional credit, for example a welcome bonus:

```bash [curl]
curl -X POST $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/customers/$CUS/credit_grants \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "category": "promotional", "unit": "credit", "amount": 500, "expires_at": "2027-01-01T00:00:00Z" }'
```

Paid credit always has an invoice. Send `category: "paid"`, the net `price_minor` and a
`tax_treatment`. We create the invoice, and the grant starts when the invoice is paid. If you
already have the money, add `mark_paid` and the grant is active in the response:

```json
{
  "category": "paid",
  "unit": "credit",
  "amount": 1000,
  "price_minor": 900,
  "tax_treatment": "taxed_at_purchase",
  "mark_paid": { "method": "bank_transfer", "reference": "Transfer 0042" }
}
```

A paid grant needs an API key with `credits:manage` and `invoices:manage`.

## Check before each model call

Ask the access check with the meter and an estimate of the cost in minor units. It writes nothing:

```bash [curl]
curl "$TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/customers/$CUS/access?meter=ai_tokens&estimate=25" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "object": "access_check",
  "allowed": true,
  "code": "ok",
  "currency": "USD",
  "available_minor": 1240,
  "available_units": [{ "unit": "credit", "units": 1240 }],
  "balance": {
    "grants_minor": 1500,
    "unbilled_usage_minor": 210,
    "open_holds_minor": 50,
    "overdraft_limit_minor": 0
  },
  "on_exhausted": { "policy": "block", "source": "business_default" }
}
```

The answer is always 200. Read `allowed`, and `code` when it is false:

| `code` | Means |
|---|---|
| `ok` | Go ahead. |
| `no_subscription` | No live subscription has a price on this meter. Paid credit does not change this answer. |
| `not_entitled` | The plan does not include the feature, or its limit is used up. |
| `spend_limit_reached` | A spend limit stops more usage. `limits` names it and when it resets. See [Spend limits and automatic top-up](/help/developers/recipes/spend-limits-and-auto-top-up). |
| `in_flight_limit` | The open holds plus `estimate` are more than the part of the balance that holds can use (`credit_config.in_flight_share_bps`, default 50%). It can occur with no hold open, and it is checked before `insufficient_credit`. |
| `insufficient_credit` | The customer's policy blocks, and the credit is used up. |

::warning
**A customer who has only a pack needs a subscription too.** The access check answers
`no_subscription` until a live subscription of the customer has a price on the meter, even with
paid credit on the balance. Subscribe every customer to a plan with the meter's usage price, for
example a USD 0 Free plan at signup, and sell packs to them on top.
::

If your app knows the customer only by a usage key, call
`GET /v1/businesses/$BIZ/access?customer_key=workspace-4821&meter=ai_tokens`.

### When the access check blocks

Blocking is off until you turn it on. With it off, `insufficient_credit` never occurs and all
usage bills on the invoice. Turn it on with `credit_config.blocking_enabled` on the business
(in the dashboard: **Settings → Credits**). The PATCH replaces the whole `credit_config`, so send
every key you want to keep, and the key needs `credits:manage`:

```bash [curl]
curl -X PATCH $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "credit_config": { "blocking_enabled": true, "on_exhausted_default": "block", "in_flight_share_bps": 10000 } }'
```

`in_flight_share_bps` is the part of the balance that open holds can use, in basis points
(default 5000 = 50%). Under a block policy, a hold or an access check with `estimate` is refused
with `in_flight_limit` when the open holds plus the amount are more than that part. With a small
balance and a fixed cost per action, 50% leaves half the balance unusable: a customer with 1.5
credits cannot start a 1-credit action. Set `10000` when holds are short and each action has a
known cost.

Then the first of these sets the policy:

1. an override on the customer (`credit_settings.on_exhausted`), until its `override_expires_at`;
2. a live grant for the meter with `on_exhausted: "block"`;
3. the `on_exhausted` of the plan price's `grants_credit`;
4. the business default, `credit_config.on_exhausted_default`.

An overdraft limit lets the balance go below zero before the block, for example USD 2.00 for a
trusted customer.

## Hold, then capture or release

Many calls can run at the same time. A check alone lets two calls see the same credit. A hold
reserves the estimate first:

```bash [curl]
curl -X POST $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/customers/$CUS/credit_holds \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "amount_minor": 25, "meter": "ai_tokens", "expires_in": 120, "scope": { "workspace": "workspace-4821" } }'
```

A refused hold answers **402** with `code: "in_flight_limit"` or `"insufficient_credit"`. Do not
make the model call.

After the call, capture the hold with the usage event. We record the event and close the hold in
one step, so the balance never counts both or neither:

```bash [curl]
curl -X POST $TMBILLING_URL/v1/businesses/$BIZ/credit_holds/$HOLD/capture \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TMBILLING_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "usage_event": {
      "key": "msg_XXXXXXXXXXXX01:output",
      "customer": "workspace-4821",
      "event_name": "ai_tokens",
      "properties": { "model": "small-v1", "token_type": "output", "tokens": 1200 }
    }
  }'
```

If the call fails, release the hold with `POST …/credit_holds/$HOLD/release`. A hold that you do
not capture or release expires after `expires_in` seconds (default 60, at most 86,400).

Usage above the hold is accepted, because the tokens were used. If it takes the balance past the
overdraft limit, the hold has `overdraft: true` and the rest bills on the next invoice.

::note
Hold events (`credit_hold_created`, `credit_hold_captured`, …) go only to webhook endpoints that
list them by name. An endpoint that receives all events does not get them, so a busy app does
not send one webhook for each model call.
::

## See every movement

`GET /v1/businesses/$BIZ/customers/$CUS/credit_transactions` lists each grant, draw, expiry, void,
restore and rollover, newest first. Filter by `grant`, `kind`, `category`, `unit`, `invoice` and
`created[gte]` / `created[lt]`. On an invoice, `grant_credits_pre_tax_minor` and
`grant_credits_post_tax_minor` show what the grants paid. In the dashboard, open the customer and
select **View credits**.

## See your cost per credit

To know what one credit costs you, send your cost on each usage event: add up what the model
calls behind one action cost, and send the total as `cost` (with `currency`). See
[See your margin](/help/developers/recipes/bill-ai-tokens#see-your-margin).

::note
For usage that credits pay, the margin report's revenue is the usage line at the price's rate per
unit. It is not what the customer paid for the credits, so a pack sold at a discount or a free
grant does not lower it. Compare it with your pack sales until the report includes them.
::
