Two Minute Billing runs recurring billing end to end: a catalog of products and prices, subscriptions with
trials and proration, invoices with tax and discounts, collection through your own payment
gateways, and dunning when a card fails.

- [Introduction](/help/developers/getting-started/introduction) — what the engine owns, and the three facts to
  know before you build.
- [Quickstart](/help/developers/getting-started/quickstart) — a test API key, then a business, a price, a
  customer and a paid subscription, in TypeScript or curl.
- [Test and live mode](/help/developers/getting-started/test-and-live) — the two modes of every business, test
  cards for every failure path, and what it takes to go live.
- [SDKs and tools](/help/developers/getting-started/sdks) — the typed client, and the OpenAPI document you can
  generate your own from.
- [Self-host with Docker](/help/developers/getting-started/self-host-docker) — run the engine, the
  worker and the dashboard on your own server with one Compose file.
