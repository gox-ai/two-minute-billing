Work through these in order. By the end you will have a working catalogue, a paying customer and
a business you can switch to live.

- [What Two Minute Billing does](/help/getting-started/what-it-does) — what it handles for you, and what it leaves to your payment provider.
- [Create your business](/help/getting-started/create-your-business) — the settings everything else depends on.
- [Build your catalogue](/help/getting-started/build-your-catalogue) — products, prices and how you charge for them.
- [Take your first payment](/help/getting-started/first-payment) — a customer, a subscription and an invoice, in test mode.
- [Go live](/help/getting-started/go-live) — the checklist before you charge a real card.
