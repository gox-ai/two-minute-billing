Flow change · downgrade & item-removal credit

The one path we are changing. Same net ledger effect on both sides — only the artifact (and one debit account) changes.

● current — as-built

Credit becomes a negative invoice

The downgrade mints a negative-total invoice with status='credited' and posts to the refundable bucket. It pollutes the invoice list and has no credit-note document.

subscriptions · changeSubscription (net < 0)
invoicing · insert invoice status=credited, total < 0
ledger · dr revenue / cr refundable_credit_balance

proration.ts:293-365 · invoicing/service.ts:754-777

● target — feature-complete billing

Credit becomes a credit note

Issue a real credit note against the current-term invoice (adjustment if unpaid, refundable if paid). The source invoice stays a normal positive invoice.

subscriptions · issueCurrentTermCredit (net < 0)
credit-notes · issueAdjustment | issueRefundable CN
ledger · dr credit_adjustments / cr refundable_credit_balance

reuses the shipped change_term_end template · proration.ts:472-499

This is row H1/H2 of the reconciliation matrix in docs/PARITY.md. Fixing it also corrects the paid/unpaid split (H2) and removes the credit-only invoice artifact (M3).
